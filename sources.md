## Considered and dropped

- **Harris and Raviv (1988)**: Grossman and Hart carry the one-share-one-vote theory alone.
- **Johnson, La Porta, Lopez-de-Silanes and Shleifer (2000), "Tunneling"**: the term's origin, but Bertrand et al. and Bae et al. carry the measured cases.
- **Aggarwal, Eldar, Hochberg and Litov (2022)**: the rise of dual-class IPOs; carried qualitatively by CII and CRS.
- **Nenova (2003)**: Dyck and Zingales carry vote-value measurement.
- **Almeida, Park, Subrahmanyam and Wolfenzon (2011)**: chaebol structure; Bae et al. suffice.
- **Autor, Dorn, Katz, Patterson and Van Reenen (2020)**: superstar concentration is adjacent, not load-bearing.
- **Easterbrook and Fischel (1991)**: the contractual turn is carried by Jensen and Meckling plus Seligman's history.
- **Chen (2016), Temasek model**: Ang and Ding plus the Fifth Schedule suffice.
- **Reuters on the Toyota unwind; KOSPI August 2026; Tata Sons**: market color, secondary or unverifiable; dropped.
- **Coinbase, DoorDash, Robinhood, Comcast filings**: real, redundant with the six specimens.
- **SEBI ICDR Regulations (2018)**: the promoter-naming point carried by NSE tracker and OECD.
- **Hirschman is retained; Friston, Sloterdijk and the seed's wider constellation**: not present in this seed; no analogous cuts needed.
